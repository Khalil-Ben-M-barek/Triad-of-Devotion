# Triad of Devotion Guide
## Boss Behavior & Mind Control
 * **Targeting:** The boss attacks a random hero every turn.
 * **Mind Control:** The boss can seize control of a hero, turning them against the party.
 * **Breaking Control:** To regain control of a mind-controlled hero, the player must successfully **block** one attack.
## Defending & The Block System
 * **How to Block:** When a hero is attacked, a specific key prompt appears over them. The player must press **only** that specific key during a specific window to block.
 * **The Gauge Trade-off:** It is not recommended to always block, as blocking prevents the damage that fills the **Potential Gauge**. The player should prioritize blocking when using *Protect Stance* to gain **Synergy Bars**.
 * **Protect Stance:** If a protected character is attacked and the player successfully blocks, **both** the protector and the protected hero gain a Synergy Bar.
## Synergy Abilities
Once enough Synergy Bars are accumulated between two heroes, the **Synergy Abilities Menu** unlocks.
 * **Effect:** Deals significant damage and grants a unique perk to both performers, varying by Synergy Ability: *Potential Level* increase, or 0 MP for the next spell the hero performs.
## Potential Levels & Potential Breaches
 * **Potential Breaches:** Deal significant damage or have unique effects and are only accessible when a hero's **Potential Gauge** is full. Higher Potential Levels unlock stronger Potential Breaches.
 * **The Cost:** Using a Potential Breach depletes the gauge and drops the hero's *Potential Level* by 1 (it cannot drop below Level 1).
## Spells, Enablers, & Preparation
 * **Spells:** Spells are tied to specific **Enablers** and consume MP to use.
 * **Pre-Battle Setup:** Players can adjust their **Enabler loadouts** and **turn order** before the battle begins.
 * **Strategy:** Every hero possesses a *Unique Ability*. Players should adjust turn order and Enablers based on each hero's Unique Ability to maximize their chance of winning.
 * **Recovery:** HP and MP can be recovered through specific Enablers and Potential Breaches (though it is significantly harder to recover MP).
